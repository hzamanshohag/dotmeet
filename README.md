## Overview

**DotMeet** is a modern platform designed for professionals, individuals, service providers, and appointment-based businesses.

Users can create and manage a **personal portfolio website** or a **business website**, showcase their services, manage appointments, interact with customers, collect reviews, and use an **AI-powered assistant** to provide automated customer support and generate potential leads.

DotMeet is designed for professionals and businesses such as:

**Doctors, teachers, lawyers, dentists, tutors, freelancers, consultants, designers, agencies, salons, fitness trainers, coaches**, and other service-based professionals.

The platform combines:

- Personal portfolio websites
- Business landing pages and websites
- Appointment management
- AI-powered customer assistance
- Smart conversation management
- AI lead generation
- Reviews and customer feedback
- Subscription management
- Customizable templates
